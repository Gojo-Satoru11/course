What is HTML?

HTML is an acronym of 'Hyper Text Markup Language' it is used to make the skeleton of your website , the website is made of 3 elements:

  1. HTML: which is the skeleton of your website
  2. CSS: like the decorations of your website
  3. JavaScript: is adding the functionality to your website

What is CSS?

CSS is an acronym of 'Cascade Style Sheet' it is used to make some styles, decorations your website, you can apply CSS styles by one of the following:

  1. inline styling
  2. internal styling
  3. external styling
